Price tracker

Track any item from a search ("Track price" on the result). Once a day DupeLens searches again, the model finds the same item, and the lowest price is recorded. You get a buy-now-or-wait hint and an alert when the price drops 5% or reaches your target.

Each check is one fresh search per item (at most once a day).

How the advice works

A simple rule on the prices recorded so far, not a trained forecaster. Under 3 days: collecting. At or within 2% of the lowest price seen: buy now. Falling trend (least-squares slope of the daily lowest price over the last 14 days, more than 0.2% a day): wait. Otherwise: how far it is above its low. With 7 or more days the dashed line shows where the straight-line trend points in a week. Telegram alerts work when a bot token is added to the server's .env.
