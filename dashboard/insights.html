PCA product map: every offer from every search

How this map is made

Each offer's photo is read by CLIP (512 numbers) and its title by MPNet (768 numbers). The matching model uses PCA, fitted on 24,065 Shopee training listings, to compress each to 32 numbers (shrinkage-whitened), giving 64 numbers per offer. Here a second PCA over all offers keeps the 2 directions with the most variation, so each offer becomes a point. Points close together look alike to the model. PCA is unsupervised: it never saw the category colours.
