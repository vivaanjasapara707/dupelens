Find the real best price.

Type any product. DupeLens collects offers from Indian online stores, uses a trained machine-learning model to keep only the listings that are exactly the same item (not a look-alike, a different colour or a different pack), and shows you the cheapest place to buy it.

How it works

Machine learning decides which listings are the same product; price is only compared afterwards.

1Collect offers

Your search is sent to Google Shopping (India) through the Serper service. It returns up to 40 offers from stores like Amazon, Flipkart, Croma and Reliance Digital, with title, photo, price and link.

2Read title and photo

Three pretrained neural networks turn every photo and title into numbers: CLIP (what the photo shows), DINOv2 (which exact object), MPNet (what the title means), plus a colour profile and a photo fingerprint.

3Judge every pair

Our logistic-regression model, trained on 34,250 Shopee listings, scores each pair of offers: same item, same item in another colour/size/pack, or a different product. Price is never an input.

4Best price

Only the offers the model calls the exact same item are compared. You see the cheapest store, the typical (median) price and how much each store charges above it.

Prices and links come live from Google Shopping (India) at search time. Matching uses DupeLens's own model (Shopee validation F1 0.864). On a held-out Amazon India test, 33 of 40 "same product" verdicts were correct (labelled by Claude, an AI assistant, not humans). Details and limits: how we tested the model.
